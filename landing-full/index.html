What is Schedule C?

Schedule C (Form 1040) is the IRS form used by sole proprietors and single-member LLCs to report profit or loss from a business. If you run your own business and file taxes as an individual—not through a corporation or partnership—you're almost certainly a Schedule C filer.

Bookkeeping for Schedule C owners typically involves:

  • Categorizing income and expenses per IRS guidelines
  • Tracking deductible business expenses (home office, supplies, mileage, etc.)
  • Separating personal and business transactions
  • Preparing clean records for tax filing and year-end returns

The Problem

Schedule C owners face unique bookkeeping challenges that generic tools don't solve well:

  • Tax categorization is confusing and time-consuming — IRS categories don't always match how you think about your expenses.
  • Deductible expenses are easy to miss or misclassify — One wrong category can cost you at tax time.
  • Many tools are built for larger businesses — Invoicing, payroll, and multi-user features you don't need.
  • Year-end tax prep is chaotic without clean records — Scrambling in April is avoidable.
